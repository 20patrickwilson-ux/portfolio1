Alpine Pursuits

Downhill Skiing & Mountain Terrain

Skiing tests balance, line choice, and mental composure in cold, fast-changing mountain environments.

Navigating the Slopes

Whether charging down steep groomers in Vermont or exploring ridge lines with friends, skiing has always been an outlet where physical focus meets total presence.

"Skiing forces instant problem-solving. At speed, you don't overthink your next turn—you commit to your line and react to the snow beneath you."

The discipline required on icy Northeast hardpack parallels competitive track & field: keeping your center of gravity disciplined, driving through your edges, and trusting hours of repetition.

Patience & Environment

Freshwater Angling & Conservation

Spending quiet mornings along local riverbanks and lakes taught me more about observation and persistence than any standard textbook could.

Reading the Water

Fishing is equal parts ecological study and patience. Identifying structure, submerged fallen timber, water temperature shifts, and seasonal habits is required before making a single cast.

"It’s not just about what you pull out of the water; it’s about understanding the health of the local watershed and learning to embrace stillness."

This passion directly ties into my ongoing volunteer cleanups with the Northern Valley Greenway Project, helping protect the local waterways and green spaces around Bergen County for the future.
